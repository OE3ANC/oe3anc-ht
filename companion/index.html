KISS modem · APRS

1200-baud AFSK on the radio’s current FM channel. Select KISS in the radio’s Modem menu before opening a standalone port, or enter from a matching companion connection. Physical PTT exits KISS. No automatic beacons, retries or message acknowledgements.

Receive first: select the intended FM channel and enable KISS, connect below, then watch Received packets. No packet is sent by connecting. RX audio is muted.

Serial port closed · radio mode unknown

Disconnected. KISS has no handshake or delivery acknowledgements.

After physical PTT or a radio settings change, close this port and explicitly reconnect. The browser cannot detect the radio’s mode through KISS.

          Cable and bootloader notes
          
Physical PTT shares the serial RX pin. Automatic DTR/RTS reset is not enabled. Connecting loads a helper into RAM without changing flash. These tools work independently of the installed application version.

          
Keep power and cable connected until each operation completes. Unplug before rebooting into normal operation.

Factory calibration

          
Read-only power tables. PWM duty is not measured RF power.

          

            
            
          

          

            Region details
            
Tables: the 64 KiB journal at 0x3B0000–0x3C0000. Raw download: the entire factory region to the end of flash. Both reads are verified.
